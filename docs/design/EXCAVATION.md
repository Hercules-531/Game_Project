# M36 — Delegated excavation

The GDD's intent is a job, not manual rock steering. A Goliath fitted with the
existing drill arm reserves a surface staging site while the operator marks a
straight next-hub route or a rectangular room footprint on the excavation panel.
The hull remains vulnerable at that worksite; its real tender/pylon supplies
the whole paid load. Completion adds the finished underground volume.

Engineering choices: a one-metre construction grid, floor level Y=-6m, tunnel
width 4/6/8/10m, room width/depth 4–20m in two-metre steps, height 2.4–3.2m
(the single-pass drill reach). Routes connect the immediately northern held hub
from an already connected underground hub. Rooms center on connected held hubs.
No held pad, no job; moving a relay away cannot create a new destination.

Only newly cleared volume is charged: 0.025 scrap/m³ rounded up and 0.4 powered
seconds/m³, minimum 60 seconds. Drill condition scales work rate using the
existing 0–100 condition bands. These are tunable values, not measured pacing.
Room expansions cannot shrink already excavated width/depth/height. Multiple
workers may take non-overlapping jobs, but a hull cannot own two jobs or build
a prefab while assigned.

Fresh tender/pylon payment credits are consumed once per work step. Power,
tool, staging or normal-link loss holds progress. A job preloaded before an
aurora counts as local scripted intent for its temporary ambient-power exploit;
it gains no permanent battery energy. On ordinary link loss the fitted failsafe
can still retrace; the job waits for its worker to regain link and staging.
Cancellation requires a linked worker and refunds no spent resources. A destroyed
worker loses its unfinished assignment. Reload preserves payment and remaining
work, while stale power credits are discarded.

Finished cells generate closed floors, roofs and outer walls, with shared room/
tunnel boundaries removed. The reviewed surfaces reuse the verified PSX stone,
concrete and metal textures. Staged review lighting is diagnostic; paid wiring,
rail service and passenger access follow M37–M39. Underground traffic/drilling
trace and entrance forcing are integrated in M40.
