# M30 — trace and independent nest clocks

Engineering choices under the user's authorization to continue without review gates. The GDD's finite strip, northward danger/reward and independent nest growth remain authoritative.

- Six 100m sectors track 0–100 trace independently. No trace is generated inside the bunker. Trace decays 0.015 points per second; actual moving vibration adds `reach / 300` per second and thermal bloom adds `reach / 500` per second. Each sonar pulse adds `reach / 100`; each transmitted radio pulse adds `reach / 250`. Repeated deployments add one point to their arrival sector. Readings describe the operator's own emissions, not omniscient enemy detections.
- Signal shielding is a real utility socket commitment: 8kg, 0.04kW, 18 scrap and 60 fabrication seconds. It reduces radio emission reach and its trace to 20%, while leaving sound/heat intact. Silent movement's existing reduced vibration contributes correspondingly less trace.
- One marquee nest per sector, off the pylon spine: (48,-99), (-43,-180), (43,-278), (-42,-370), (40,-470), (-40,-578). Native low-poly stone-textured organic clumps are rendered for review before use. Final band layouts and rewards are M42 content, not fabricated here.
- Independent growth intervals from south to north: 3600, 3300, 3000, 2700, 2400, 2100 seconds. Initial growth 0,1,1,2,2,3; capped at six. Each nest has its own remaining clock and reinforcement countdown. Quiet sectors retain baseline growth; trace thresholds 30 and 60 add patrol pressure, with at most six active nest-owned scouts per sector.
- Reinforcements are physical scouts spawned at clear points within 5m of their nest, checked against actual collision volumes. A blocked spawn retries later. Dead scouts do not count as active pressure; their spent bodies are removed at the next local reinforcement cycle. Spawn serials persist, preventing identity reuse on reload.
- A nest at growth four, or growth two with trace 80+, deduces home and redirects its real patrols toward the exterior airlock. There is no teleport, remote damage, or bunker ingress here. Coordinated sieges/defenses are M33; physical breaches are M40.
- A destroyed nest stops growth and recruitment permanently, while surviving patrols remain threats. Clearing/suppression tools arrive with defenses. Integrity is 200 +100 per northward sector. Current low-tier sensors provide geometry/anonymous warmth only. Exact nest readings become available only after a powered linked drone physically surveys within 8m and clear reach.
- Survey collection additionally requires functional optics, the CCD's real 70°/4:3 view frustum and unobstructed sight. Visible mass growth and state are cached; patrol count includes only living scouts inside that camera frustum and clear reach. Lost links or turned cameras retain stale readings. Signal shielding reuses the already-reviewed circuit-cover mesh and PSX metal material.
- Trace, sortie records, nest growth clocks, reinforcement timers, home discovery, hull populations, damage and discoveries survive save/load. Pause freezes them. Save v22 migrates older runs to their initial nests with zero trace.

These are tuning values, not an assertion that the 40-hour target is met.
