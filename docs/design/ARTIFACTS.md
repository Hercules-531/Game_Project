# M34 — Universal artifact sockets

The nine context-dependent effects in GDD section 07 are implemented directly.
Each unique device occupies one universal socket: one per hull, one in the
breaker panel. They are never manufactured or duplicated. The three native
anomalous devices use verified PSX metal/stone textures and are reviewed before
playable acceptance. Human implantation stays outside the current GDD scope.

Each find weighs 1kg, requires real local cargo retrieval and unloading, then
can be fitted or removed at the home workshop/gantry. Fitted mass counts against
hull payload. A physical device left on a stranded hull stays with that hull.
Transfer never restores cells, clears damage or credits scrap.

Small context means an internally powered hull; heavy context means a Goliath
that ordinarily requires external power. The anomaly itself is passive in an
intact fitted hull, including a stranded one; it does not power motors except
for the explicitly specified photonic trickle. Destruction disables hull effects.

| Device | Internal-cell hull | Externally powered heavy | Bunker breaker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal Siphon | No cold drain; continuous running/tool vibration suppressed, while deliberate sonar and weapon reports remain audible | 10m heat ring removes local cold drain and thaws frozen ground, with solid heat occlusion | Heating electricity cost multiplied by 0.7, heating effectiveness unchanged |
| Neural Interface Core | Crisp 640x480, 60Hz first-person feed for manual precision control | Allows normal direct piloting; optics still require their fitted hardware | Rule evaluation at 36Hz versus normal 12Hz: 200% faster = triple rate, with unchanged motor speed |
| Photonic Converter | Continuous 0.05kW cell trickle from ambient light/radiation, capped at capacity | Offsets 20% of required umbilical power; a tender or pylon remains necessary | Separate 0.25kW auxiliary output, day and night, additive to solar |

Find sites are authored at E-16/N170 (thermal), E24/N360 (neural), E-18/N485
(photonic). They establish recovery locations; final routes and rewards are
authored in the later band checkpoints. A local frozen patch provides a physical
thaw scenario; it multiplies travel speed by 0.6 while cold and clears locally within the heat
ring. The quantitative rates and sites are tunable engineering choices, not
claims of final balance or a measured 40-hour playthrough.

A stranded intact heavy siphon still radiates detectable heat and leaves trace.
Its passive warmth does not grant motor power or erase the recovery problem.
