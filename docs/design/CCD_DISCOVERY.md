# First optical hardware

M19 preserves the opening support camera while placing the Mule's first CCD in a
recoverable case at (4,-62), in the approach. Both are Tier 1: 240×180, 15 FPS,
monochrome, static/scanline loss, highlights bloomed and shadows suppressed. The
GDD's first-camera discovery means the blind salvage platform earns its own eyes;
it does not upgrade the logistics camera to an unspecified tier.

Recovery requires a deployed machine within 3m, winch or rack, and 4kg free payload.
The case follows its carrier; only docking unloads one 65%-condition CCD into stock.
It can then be physically fitted in the workshop. Delivery itself grants no vision.
No CCD fabrication recipe exists. State/owner persist in version 6, with ownership
and cargo validation. Older saves migrate with the unclaimed case.

CCD renders its own SubViewport once per sensor tick. The main simulation/camera
remain independent. Blind machines never create that camera. Hardware removal
destroys the viewport. Workshop fitting/repair now reject field or transferring
machines so reconfiguration cannot happen remotely for free.
