# Derelict recovery

Two 300 kg pre-collapse tracked frames sit at (10,-88) and (-12,-125).
Their joints/cell/armor begin at 55/60/42%; sonar/IR/heavy winch at 45/60/55%.
The tracked recovery chassis appears before M28a to make this GDD choice
playable. M28a still adds its fabrication, terrain advantages and scraper.

Workshop recipes provide a 12-scrap jumper and 14-scrap torch. A jumper
consumes up to 0.25 kWh from the donor, retaining 0.05 kWh, with 90% transfer
efficiency within 3 m. Claiming the frame preserves its configuration.
The same coupler revives an existing stranded fleet member.

A tracked chassis with an operational size-two heavy winch can pull the
300 kg frame. It travels at 25% speed, adds an acoustic/work load and pays
extra power. The physical target follows behind a visible 2.5 m cable
through collision physics; a 6 m stretch or three-second blockage releases
it. Both bodies must reach the local airlock cradle to recover the frame.
The recovered frame then needs its own normal loader cycle.

A torch requires 20 kg free payload and 20 seconds beside the frame.
It packs the original modules and their condition into cargo, then leaves
the stripped frame in place. Stock credit happens once at home.

Version 12 persists decisions, paid energy through drone cells, stripped
cargo, cutting time, the target body, cable owner and blockage timer.
These constants are documented balance choices under the user's current
authorization, not specifications added to the GDD.
