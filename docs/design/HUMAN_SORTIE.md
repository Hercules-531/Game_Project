# Emergency human recovery

The suit locker is a verified PSX box beside the inner airlock. It unlocks
when every fleet member is deployed and either empty or beyond radio range.
A home machine remains recoverable through the workshop/charger, so it does
not force a sortie. Destruction integrates with the later threat milestone.

Suiting consumes five scrap for a filter, 2% bunker oxygen and 0.30 kWh
from the grid's battery. The analog visor carries 480 seconds oxygen,
300 seconds surface filter and a finite jumper. Oxygen counts throughout
the outing; filters count only on the surface. Speed is 1.15 m/s.
These are balance choices under the current autonomous authorization.

The operator cycles the real interlocked doors with added outer controls.
There is no sortie teleport. E on a dead machine transfers up to 0.25 kWh
from the suit at 90% efficiency, with a 3 m reach and solid-cover check.
The revived machine follows the operator through collision steering.
Blocked routes require the operator to lead around them. When the operator
enters the airlock, the escort waits at the exterior loader cradle.
Stow the suit at home and dock the machine from operations to resume work.

Oxygen or filter exhaustion locks the death menu and reloads the last save,
using the user's approved failure consequence. Death cannot overwrite that
save. A sortie can be saved with its gauges, authorization and escort intact.
Combat evaluation, additional environmental filter costs and audible suit
urgency belong to the threat/weather/audio checkpoints.

The visor's analog needles, edge frame and light scratches are drawn in code;
there is no new bitmap asset or improvised texture path.
