# Advanced hull choices

Implemented sequentially under the user's authorization to complete checkpoints
without further review questions. Custom forms use verified PSX metal/radio
textures, with live rendered inspection before acceptance.

M28a retains the M24b tracked wedge. Its frame costs 70 scrap / 180 seconds;
heavy winch 32 / 90, powered tread scraper 12 / 45, passive sealed housing
14 / 45. A single utility socket makes scraping, sealing and other support a
loadout choice. Track slope limit 42 degrees versus light hull 25 degrees.
The authored E24/N64 incline is 30 degrees; its convex collision prism has a
ground-level toe. Foot adaptation uses clear vertical and horizontal sweeps and
a walkable floor probe, capped at 18cm for tracks / 35cm for light quadrupeds.
It cannot lift through walls/ceilings or over a slope above the hull limit.

Deep sludge at E-24/N53 reduces tracked speed to 20%, or 85% with working
scrapers. Sealed housings reduce weather exposure odds to 40% and remain passive
during a circuit short. No absolute environmental immunity is granted.
Newly assembled cells start empty and use paid grid charging; the opening pair
retains its GDD starting charge. This closes a fabrication energy loophole.

M28b: Goliath frame 160 scrap / 360s, tender 90 / 240s; coupler 20 / 60s,
field collector 24 / 90s, structure winch 70 / 180s, drill 80 / 180s.
Goliath payload 2500kg, speed 0.65m/s, 20kW idle plus 12kW full travel;
size-3 tools add 6–8kW. Its 0.2kWh internal reserve cannot operate the chassis.
It normally requires standing orders; direct piloting is the later neural effect.
Tender has a 12kWh paid battery bank, two size-2 utilities and independent motion.

Connect within 4m using a powered, radio-linked tender with a free coupler.
The physical line breaks beyond 6m or across solid obstruction. A 64kW port
supplies actual heavy draw at 90% conversion; payment precedes heavy physics.
The receiver never accumulates cable energy. Low-cell abort uses donor reserve.
Portable collectors add 1kW baseline / 1.75kW sunlight through an actual sun ray.
Distance, shorts or an empty bank strand the heavy frame. A human must wake the
tender rather than trying to jumper an unsupported Goliath.

The heavy frame assembles outside at E4/N10 and stays outside. Local gantry fitting,
service and eight-second unloading provide workshop access without forcing it
through the small airlock. Unloading draws 0.6kW through fresh grid payment and
cannot progress during blackout. Mid-transfer saves remain rejected. Structure
winch accepts prefab-scale cargo up to available payload; actual prefab sites are M32.
The drill is hardware here; delegated excavation starts in M36.
