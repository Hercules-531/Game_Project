# M35 — Earned multispectrum optics

The recovered optics archive and its existing prerequisites unlock fabrication
of a size-1 sensor module: 10kg, 0.8kW, 80 scrap, 180 fabrication seconds.
This is 20 times the old CCD's operating draw and uses a real sensor socket.
Its existing condition/repair and weather exposure rules still apply.

Working optics provide a clean 640x480 feed at 60Hz, local night-vision processing,
1x/2x/4x digital zoom, and automatic classification tags for actually visible,
unoccluded landmarks and organic hostiles. Identification range is 80m scaled
down by poor lens condition; a failing lens also reduces frame cadence. Night
vision raises the received low-light image; it does not see through solid cover
or create geometry in complete darkness. Zoom crops the received image and
narrows the corresponding tag/frustum bounds. These display controls are local
processing, not new movement/radio-immunity abilities.

Link loss, empty cells and sensor faults withhold fresh image/tags. A small
Neural Core remains a separate clean-camera source without free multispectrum
classification, night vision or zoom. Unknown/hidden targets do not appear in
the tag list. Tags report class and measured range, not enemy health or identity.
View mode and zoom persist per hull without granting missing hardware.
The compact feed labels at most three contacts at once, prioritizing organic
hostiles and then nearest objects; rubble is left to the image. Label placement
avoids overlap while the full local sensor result remains bounded to 16 contacts.

The existing reviewed camera housing receives an additional low-poly PSX metal
optical head. No new bitmap assets. Rates, prices and range are tunable engineering
values under the user's authorization to proceed; human readability/pacing is
still to be assessed.
