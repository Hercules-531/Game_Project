# Blind sensing

M18: sonar casts 72 horizontal physical rays to 60m when pinged. It has a 2s
cooldown; returns expire after 8s and never refresh just because a drone moves.
The polar display reprojects last-known world echoes relative to the hull. No
object identity is transmitted. These are provisional sensor ranges/cadences.

IR samples a 90-degree forward cone to 35m at 2Hz. Only warm emitters above the
heat threshold appear as ambiguous gradients; ordinary geometry/textures and
identities are absent. Physical concrete blocks readings. The warm vent is visible;
cold pylons/rubble are not. Sensor removal immediately stops new acquisition.
Blind hulls contain no ordinary camera. Ping emission is exposed for later trace
and audio integration, separate from its visual display.
