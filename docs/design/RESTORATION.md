# M42a — Restoration checklist and ending

The authorized campaign uses six 100m bands. Restoration reads the existing
physical world; it creates no territory, patrol deaths, fences or resources.

Engineering choices under continuing authorization:

- Every band must be held, its marquee nest cleared or permanently suppressed,
  and all five sections of its paid perimeter must still have positive health.
  Fence electricity is a useful countermeasure, not an extra win requirement.
- Residual density is living organic actors divided by six 100m bands, with a
  strict target below 0.5 per 100m (at most two survivors). Dead bodies do not
  count. Clearing a nest does not erase surviving patrols. The restoration panel
  reports aggregate pressure and each band checklist, without enemy locations,
  unseen patrol counts or nest growth/health. This is the GDD's restoration
  instrument; detailed nest intel remains subject to optical surveys.
- The existing permanent cleared/suppressed states satisfy the marquee item.
  Clearance is currently earned with the observed defense fire system; this
  checkpoint does not add a new suppression tool or simulate completion.
- Completing all items while the operator is alive latches restoration once,
  saves it, pauses simulation and opens credits. Returning to operations never
  clears the achievement. Save v36 retains completion time and whether the
  automatic credits were presented; Continue does not replay acknowledged
  credits. An interrupted, unacknowledged ending can present once on reload.
  Failed persistence is visible and retryable; it never claims the ending saved.
- The checklist is available from the command terminal. Six individual band
  content milestones follow M42a: band_01.tres through band_06.tres. Each gets
  layout/reward/danger checks and a native route/landmark review before commit.

Files: restoration tracker, checklist and credits scenes/scripts, session/menu
flow, schema v36, main scene, fleet service entry, tests and evidence.
Verify each missing criterion independently, strict density boundary and bodies,
separate per-band requirements, real nest damage/perimeter loss, exactly-once
credits, pause/settings/free play, save/reload/migration, failed-save retry and
640×480 layout. Authored content and the human 40-hour pacing test remain later
milestones; a controlled completion fixture does not prove campaign playtime.
