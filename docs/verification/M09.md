# M09 — PSX bunker art

Live MCP audit: 147 world mesh nodes, zero materials outside the PS1 shader.
18 unique texture paths, all inside the PSX house pack, all 64×64 without mipmaps.
Nearest sampling, affine mapping, vertex snapping and compositor remain enabled.
Exact source map: [Bunker assets](../design/BUNKER_ASSETS.md).

Fresh in-game review:

- [Command room](M09/command.png): three CRTs, connected desk grouping, radio,
  chair and storage; no prop crosses the doorway.
- [Workshop](M09/workshop.png): larger bench, shelving and cargo staging.
- [Life support](M09/life.png): breaker housings and radiator, storage opposite.
- [Passage](M09/passage.png): two-tone walls, tiled floor, skirting, lit fixtures
  and overhead service conduits. Openings and signs remain visible.
- [Airlock](M09/airlock.png): textured metal ribs/crossbar/latch; measured sliding
  door geometry and interlock remain in service.

Full GUT suite: 11 tests, 55 assertions, all passed in 21.827 seconds. Actual
walking visits all rooms and the airlock. New bench-contact check stops the
operator at its front without lifting the capsule; no doorway is blocked.

Windows export succeeds. Both headless and real D3D12 exported runtimes complete
the process/physics verification probe (rendered: 57 process frames, 30 physics
ticks), with no development bridge or shader errors. Hosted CI remains pending.

Issues resolved before acceptance:

- Material conversion now preserves an existing ShaderMaterial when replacing
  a mesh-level override with per-surface overrides.
- Godot's dummy renderer reported a null material during whole-scene teardown
  with imported material replacements. An isolated headless reproduction showed
  it while sequential child teardown did not. GPU material replacement and
  non-colliding surface detail are now restricted to RenderingDevice runs.
  Headless tests keep imported props, identical gameplay colliders and room shell;
  art validation is explicitly performed in the live renderer, not inferred from
  those tests. The live editor and exported GPU run report no teardown errors.

This is the first bunker art pass. Station screens and mechanics follow in their
own milestones; this milestone does not claim the complete game is playable.
