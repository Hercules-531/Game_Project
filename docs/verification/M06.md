# M06 — Fixed-resolution presentation

The live Godot AI bridge ran `render_lab.tscn` at three launch sizes. All
captures were fresh (`stale_frame=false`) and contained a 320×240 framebuffer.
The one-pixel checker stays sharply alternating; no antialiasing is enabled.

| Window | Integer transform | Center offset | GUI click in calibration control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 800×600 | 2× | (80,60) | (24,6) |
| 1280×720 | 3× | (160,0) | (24,6) |
| 960×720 | 3× | (0,0) | (24,6) |

Clicks were injected in embedder coordinates through `Window.push_input` with
`in_local_coords=false`; the actual Control received the expected local point.
The engine performs coordinate conversion including the letterbox offset.
The default window is restored to 960×720. FOV remains 75 degrees.

Runtime resize requests are overridden by the editor's embedded game host;
separate launches with actual measured sizes establish the three cases above.
Screenshots capture the framebuffer, not the operating system's enlarged window;
integer enlargement and border dimensions are established by live transforms.
Camera viewpoints vary between captures due to live operator input.
